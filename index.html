Simen Aardal Ulsaker

Associate Professor, Oslo Business School, OsloMet

My research is in industrial organization and applied microeconomics, with a focus on competition, consumer behaviour, and vertical relationships. A central theme in my recent work is retail and grocery markets, where I study pricing, consumer search and price knowledge, cross-border shopping, and the competitive effects of entry and regulation.

I also work on theoretical questions related to exclusionary contracts, investment incentives, and vertical restraints. I am an associated researcher with the FOOD research project at NHH.
